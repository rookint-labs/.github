# Rook

**Business intelligence built around evidence, change and signals.**

Rook helps you understand what is happening inside and around a business by turning fragmented data into structured intelligence.

We collect information from public and private sources, track how it changes over time and surface useful signals such as:

- Hiring and team changes
- Leadership changes
- Commercial activity
- Technology changes
- Company and operational updates

## What you'll find here

This organisation contains public tools, experiments and selected components from the wider Rook platform.

Our first public tools focus on making company data easier to explore and understand, starting with UK Companies House data.

Rook is primarily built in **Go**, with PostgreSQL and local or OpenAI-compatible language models used where they add value.

## Built around evidence

Rook is designed to answer more than:

> What is true?

It also asks:

> What changed, when did it change and what evidence supports it?

That principle sits at the core of how we model observations, signals and business intelligence.
